MORE OPPORTUNITY. LESS TO ORGANISE.

A music programme your pupils can get excited about.

There is something special about a child discovering a sound they can make themselves. A first melody, a steady beat or a chord played with friends can turn a little curiosity into something they look forward to each week.

We work with your school to build a lesson service around the instruments, rooms and teaching arrangements you agree with us. Your pupils get an inviting introduction to music, with the practical running of the service managed by MML.

THE INSTRUMENTS ARE PART OF THE SERVICE

More music. Fewer things for your team to arrange.

You do not have to start by organising a set of lesson instruments. We provide them free as part of the service, and work with you to agree the practical arrangements for lessons at your school.

Explore piano, guitar, ukulele, drums and singing with us. Together, we establish which instruments and lesson formats will be offered at your school before parents are invited to choose.

A SUPPORTED TEACHING TEAM

Good lessons start with the people behind them.

Our role goes beyond arranging a teaching slot. MML manages checks including enhanced DBS, identity, right to work and professional references, alongside safeguarding training and the practical running of the service.

We want children to feel encouraged to ask questions, try something new and enjoy the small steps that make learning rewarding. Your school has a named account contact, access to our safeguarding contact and an agreed arrangement to work from.

CLEAR FOR SCHOOLS. SIMPLE FOR FAMILIES.

A joined-up experience from first enquiry to the next lesson.

Parents start on a website for your school, where they can explore the instruments and current lesson offers. Your school’s name and full address stay visible as families begin their application, so their choices relate to the right place.

Once lessons are arranged, the parent experience brings lesson information, practice guidance and communication together. Your school’s portal keeps agreed provision and service information close at hand, with requests routed to the MML team.

KEYS. STRINGS. RHYTHM. VOICE.

So many ways to find their sound.

Explore the instruments with us. The lessons available to families follow each school’s agreed provision.

Piano

The piano makes music wonderfully visible. Move along the keys to explore higher and lower sounds, find a melody and discover how notes can work together.

Guitar

A melody picked one note at a time. A first chord ringing clearly. The guitar brings rhythm, harmony and the familiar sound of a song into one instrument.

Ukulele

Compact, lively and full of character, the ukulele invites learners to explore rhythm and chords on four strings.

Drums

Drumming turns rhythm into something you can hear and feel. A steady pulse, a repeating pattern and a well-timed change can give a piece of music its energy.

Singing

Singing gives learners a direct way to explore melody, words and expression, using an instrument they carry with them.

A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

Your school’s questions, answered.

From instruments to the next step, here is how the service comes together.

What does My Music Lessons manage?

We manage the music lesson service end to end, including the lesson instruments, teacher checks, compliance and training, and the ongoing administration and communication. We agree the practical school arrangements with your team before the service starts.

Do we need to provide the instruments?

MML provides the lesson instruments free as part of the service. We will discuss what is needed for your school’s agreed programme and how lessons will use the available space.

Which instruments can we offer?

Talk to us about piano, guitar, ukulele, drums and singing. The instruments and formats offered to parents will reflect the provision agreed for your school and its current availability.

What does our school need to organise?

We need to understand your school, agree suitable rooms and teaching times, and have the right school contact to work with. MML manages the running of the lesson service from there.

How do parents sign up?

Parents use the website for your school and go directly to its signup form. Your school’s name and address remain visible as they explore the instruments offered there and review the lesson information.

Can we ask for more teaching time later?

Yes. Your school can request additional time or raise a proposed change with the MML team. The existing agreed arrangement stays clear while that request is reviewed.

How do we get started?

Tell us a little about your school through the partnership enquiry. We will discuss the opportunity, practical arrangements and next steps with you.
