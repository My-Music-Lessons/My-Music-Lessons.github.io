The playbook

Where these came from, and how one changes

A hundred and eighteen of these were not written. They were already printed on the admin screens as explanation - the business’s own rules, set as page furniture - and were harvested from them. That is why most rules name the screen they came from.

The rest were ruled on by the owner against a real case, and those name the case. A rule with no case behind it was never ruled on; a rule with one can be argued with on the facts of that case.

Nothing on this screen changes a rule. A rule changes when a situation is ruled on, with the case in front of you, and the new version lands here with what was said and why. Mel is where that happens.
