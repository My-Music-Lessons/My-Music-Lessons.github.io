Bringing a music service to a Peterborough school

We staff it, cover it and record it. Your school keeps its timetable.

Peterborough schools use us as their instrumental provision: we bring the teachers, the instruments and the records, and the school keeps its timetable.

Peterborough has 83 primary schools. Fulbridge Academy is the biggest of them at around 940 on roll, and 33 of them are the size an in-school music service usually runs in.

ONE CLEAR NEXT STEP

From a first conversation to music in a Peterborough school.

  1. 01

    One conversation

    You name the school. We find out what is already running there, what the funding allows, and what we could put on next term.

  2. 02

    A room and a slot

    You give us a room and a rotating slot. Nothing else on your timetable changes, and the rotation is what stops one subject losing out.

  3. 03

    We staff it and we cover it

    Our teachers arrive, teach one-to-one, in small groups and in bands of up to five, and we send cover when somebody is ill. Your office is not asked to find anybody.

  4. 04

    The records come back to you

    Registers, practice notes and safeguarding evidence, held by us and open to your team. We bill families directly; you keep the relationship.

WHAT EACH SIDE DOES

What we bring, and what a Peterborough school provides

Provision can grow. A school asks for more time, and the arrangement already in place carries on unchanged until a new one is agreed.

Ours to provide

The instruments
Free to the school, brought by the teacher and taken away again. Nothing to buy, nothing to store.
Checks and training
Every teacher is DBS checked to enhanced level and safeguarding trained before they join a round, not after.
A page for your school
Your own lesson page, with your name, your address and the instruments actually offered. Parents start there.
The running of it
Registers, practice notes, invoices and the messages home. A parent with a question asks us, not you.

Yours to provide

A room and a slot
A room to teach in and a slot that moves through the week. Nothing else on your timetable changes.
One person to ask
Someone who can answer a question about a room or a date. Most weeks there is nothing to ask.

In Peterborough that is the same arrangement in every school we work with, across all 83 of the primaries listed on this page.

WHO PAYS, AND WHEN

Who pays for a place

A place has one payer and never two. Which one applies at a school in Peterborough depends on that school and its funding, not on who asks first. Ask and we will tell you what is available at yours.

  • Funded places available

    A school funds a number of places, so those children pay nothing. How many depends on the school and its funding.

  • Family-paid places

    A flat monthly plan, the same every month, with missed lessons made up rather than refunded.

IN MORE DETAIL

More about the service in Peterborough

The same service, answered the way a different reader would ask about it. A business manager and a music lead are not asking the same question, and neither of them should have to read past the other one’s answer to find their own.

The music service for Peterborough

A teacher is one person on one day. A service is what happens when that person is ill, when a child moves up a year, when a parent asks what their money bought, and when somebody asks the school to account for it. We hold all four for Peterborough schools.

In Peterborough that covers 83 primary schools, and a head looking for their own will find it listed on this page.

In-school music service in Peterborough

Out of school means the families who cannot get there are out of it. In school means the child whose parents work until six learns an instrument on the same terms as everybody else, and that is most of why we run it this way in Peterborough.

The 83 primary schools listed for Peterborough come from the Department for Education register rather than from us.

School music lessons in Peterborough

One to five children at a time, inside the day. A practice note goes home afterwards, so a parent in Peterborough knows what happened without anybody in your office having to tell them.

Children in Peterborough learn Piano, Guitar, Drums, Singing and Ukulele, and the mix is settled by what they ask for in the first term rather than by us.

Peripatetic music teachers in Peterborough

Peripatetic means travelling, and the travelling is the point. A teacher on a fixed round through Peterborough has somebody else on that round already working in the next school along, so a missed week is covered rather than refunded.

Peterborough is in Cambridgeshire, and the agreement is the same in every school we work with there.

Instrumental tuition in Peterborough

Tuition rather than an activity: there is a thing to learn, a way of telling whether it is being learnt, and a note home saying what to practise before next week. That is what a Peterborough family is paying for, or what a Peterborough school is funding on their behalf.

What a Peterborough school provides is a room and a rotating slot. Where it funds places it pays for those, and there is nothing else - no cover to arrange, no instruments to buy, no invoices through your office.

Music lessons for Peterborough schools

What we ask of a Peterborough school is deliberately narrow - a room and a rotating slot - and everything after that is ours. No invoices through your office, no cover to find, no instrument cupboard to manage.

A lesson in Peterborough is one to five children, in term time, inside the school day, and a practice note goes home after it.

School band and ensemble in Peterborough

Up to five children, one room, one piece of music. A band or a small ensemble is where a child stops practising and starts playing with other people, which is the part a lesson on its own cannot give them - and in Peterborough it happens inside the school day like everything else we run.

Peterborough sits on the same weekly round as Cambridge, Huntingdon and St Neots, which is what makes cover possible rather than merely promised.
