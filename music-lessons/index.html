An in-school music service, county by county

We run instrumental teaching inside the school day, in 384 towns and cities across all 48 counties of England. Find where you are.

  • Music Hub partnerLocal music education hubs

Free, subsidised and family-paid places

  • Free placesFunded places, where the school or the hub covers the cost and the family pays nothing.
  • Subsidised placesPart-funded places, where a family pays a reduced monthly amount and the rest is covered.
  • Family-paid placesA flat monthly plan, the same every month, with missed lessons made up rather than refunded.

Which of the three applies depends on the school and its funding, not on who asks first. Ask and we will tell you what is available at yours.
