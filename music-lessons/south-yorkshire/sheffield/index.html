Teachers, instruments and cover

We staff the lessons and cover them when somebody is ill. Your school does not find a replacement.

Inside the school day

A room and a rotating slot. One-to-one, small groups, and bands of up to five, in term time, so the same subject is never missed twice.

Safeguarding and records held by us

Checks, registers, practice notes and evidence, kept by us and available to your team whenever you ask.

Two payers, and never both

A place is funded by the school or paid for by the family, never part of each. Many schools fund a set number of places and the other families pay. Which applies depends on the school, not on who asks first.

ONE CLEAR NEXT STEP

From a first conversation to music in a Sheffield school.

  1. 01

    One conversation

    You name the school. We find out what is already running there, what the funding allows, and what we could put on next term.

  2. 02

    A room and a slot

    You give us a room and a rotating slot. Nothing else on your timetable changes, and the rotation is what stops one subject losing out.

  3. 03

    We staff it and we cover it

    Our teachers arrive, teach one-to-one, in small groups and in bands of up to five, and we send cover when somebody is ill. Your office is not asked to find anybody.

  4. 04

    The records come back to you

    Registers, practice notes and safeguarding evidence, held by us and open to your team. We bill families directly; you keep the relationship.

WHAT EACH SIDE DOES

What we bring, and what a Sheffield school provides

If you want more teaching time later you ask for it, and what was agreed stays exactly as it is while the request is looked at. Nothing moves underneath you.

Ours to provide

The instruments
Free to the school, brought by the teacher and taken away again. Nothing to buy, nothing to store.
Checks and training
Enhanced DBS, identity, references and safeguarding training, done before a first lesson. We hold the evidence and show it on request.
A page for your school
Your own lesson page, with your name, your address and the instruments actually offered. Parents start there.
The running of it
Registers, practice notes, invoices and the messages home. A parent with a question asks us, not you.

Yours to provide

A room and a slot
One room and a time that rotates, so the same child never misses the same subject twice.
One person to ask
A name we can reach when a room changes. It is rarely more than a message.

A Sheffield school that wants the evidence behind any line of this can ask for it. It exists already rather than being assembled when somebody asks.

WHO PAYS, AND WHEN

Who pays for a place

A place has one payer and never two. Which one applies at a school in Sheffield depends on that school and its funding, not on who asks first. Ask and we will tell you what is available at yours.

  • Funded places available

    A school funds a number of places, so those children pay nothing. How many depends on the school and its funding.

  • Family-paid places

    A flat monthly plan, the same every month, with missed lessons made up rather than refunded.

IN MORE DETAIL

More about the service in Sheffield

The same service, answered the way a different reader would ask about it. A business manager and a music lead are not asking the same question, and neither of them should have to read past the other one’s answer to find their own.

The music service for Sheffield

A teacher is one person on one day. A service is what happens when that person is ill, when a child moves up a year, when a parent asks what their money bought, and when somebody asks the school to account for it. We hold all four for Sheffield schools.

A lesson in Sheffield is one to five children, in term time, inside the school day, and a practice note goes home after it.

In-school music service in Sheffield

Out of school means the families who cannot get there are out of it. In school means the child whose parents work until six learns an instrument on the same terms as everybody else, and that is most of why we run it this way in Sheffield.

Children in Sheffield learn Piano, Guitar, Drums, Singing and Ukulele, and the mix is settled by what they ask for in the first term rather than by us.

School music lessons in Sheffield

One to five children at a time, inside the day. A practice note goes home afterwards, so a parent in Sheffield knows what happened without anybody in your office having to tell them.

Sheffield is in South Yorkshire, and the agreement is the same in every school we work with there.

Peripatetic music teachers in Sheffield

Our teachers travel to schools across Sheffield on a fixed weekly round, which is what makes cover possible when one of them is ill - the school is never left with an empty room.

When a teacher on the Sheffield round is ill, the person who covers is already working in the next school along.

Instrumental tuition in Sheffield

Tuition rather than an activity: there is a thing to learn, a way of telling whether it is being learnt, and a note home saying what to practise before next week. That is what a Sheffield family is paying for, or what a Sheffield school is funding on their behalf.

In Sheffield that covers 166 primary schools, and a head looking for their own will find it listed on this page.

Music lessons for Sheffield schools

Provision usually fails on administration rather than on teaching. A Sheffield school gives us a room and a slot; we hold the staffing, the cover, the safeguarding evidence and the billing, and hand the registers back.

Sheffield sits on the same weekly round as Doncaster, Rotherham and Barnsley, which is what makes cover possible rather than merely promised.

School band and ensemble in Sheffield

Up to five children, one room, one piece of music. A band or a small ensemble is where a child stops practising and starts playing with other people, which is the part a lesson on its own cannot give them - and in Sheffield it happens inside the school day like everything else we run.

What a Sheffield school provides is a room and a rotating slot. Where it funds places it pays for those, and there is nothing else - no cover to arrange, no instruments to buy, no invoices through your office.
