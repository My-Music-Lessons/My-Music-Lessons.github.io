Peripatetic music teaching across Coventry

We staff it, cover it and record it. Your school keeps its timetable.

The music in a Coventry school week does not have to be an add-on. We staff it, run it and report on it as part of the ordinary day.

Coventry has 96 primary schools. Wyken Croft Primary School is the biggest of them at around 900 on roll, and 50 of them are the size an in-school music service usually runs in.

Teachers, instruments and cover

We staff the lessons and cover them when somebody is ill. Your school does not find a replacement.

Inside the school day

A room and a rotating slot. One-to-one, small groups, and bands of up to five, in term time, so the same subject is never missed twice.

Safeguarding and records held by us

Checks, registers, practice notes and evidence, kept by us and available to your team whenever you ask.

Two payers, and never both

A place is funded by the school or paid for by the family, never part of each. Many schools fund a set number of places and the other families pay. Which applies depends on the school, not on who asks first.

ONE CLEAR NEXT STEP

From a first conversation to music in a Coventry school.

  1. 01

    One conversation

    You name the school. We find out what is already running there, what the funding allows, and what we could put on next term.

  2. 02

    A room and a slot

    You give us a room and a rotating slot. Nothing else on your timetable changes, and the rotation is what stops one subject losing out.

  3. 03

    We staff it and we cover it

    Our teachers arrive, teach one-to-one, in small groups and in bands of up to five, and we send cover when somebody is ill. Your office is not asked to find anybody.

  4. 04

    The records come back to you

    Registers, practice notes and safeguarding evidence, held by us and open to your team. We bill families directly; you keep the relationship.

WHO PAYS, AND WHEN

Who pays for a place

A place has one payer and never two. Which one applies at a school in Coventry depends on that school and its funding, not on who asks first. Ask and we will tell you what is available at yours.

  • Funded places available

    A school funds a number of places, so those children pay nothing. How many depends on the school and its funding.

  • Family-paid places

    A flat monthly plan, the same every month, with missed lessons made up rather than refunded.

IN MORE DETAIL

More about the service in Coventry

The same service, answered the way a different reader would ask about it. A business manager and a music lead are not asking the same question, and neither of them should have to read past the other one’s answer to find their own.

The music service for Coventry

A teacher is one person on one day. A service is what happens when that person is ill, when a child moves up a year, when a parent asks what their money bought, and when somebody asks the school to account for it. We hold all four for Coventry schools.

What a Coventry school provides is a room and a rotating slot. Where it funds places it pays for those, and there is nothing else - no cover to arrange, no instruments to buy, no invoices through your office.

In-school music service in Coventry

Nothing happens after the bell. A child in Coventry walks to the room, has their lesson and walks back, and the only thing that changes on your timetable is that a room is booked.

A lesson in Coventry is one to five children, in term time, inside the school day, and a practice note goes home after it.

School music lessons in Coventry

Lessons one-to-one, in small groups and in bands of up to five, during the school day, with a practice note going home after each one. That is the shape of it in Coventry.

In Coventry that covers 96 primary schools, and a head looking for their own will find it listed on this page.

Peripatetic music teachers in Coventry

Our teachers travel to schools across Coventry on a fixed weekly round, which is what makes cover possible when one of them is ill - the school is never left with an empty room.

Coventry sits on the same weekly round as Birmingham, Wolverhampton and Solihull, which is what makes cover possible rather than merely promised.

Instrumental tuition in Coventry

Tuition on the instrument the child actually wants, taught by somebody who plays it, inside the Coventry school day.

Children in Coventry learn Piano, Guitar, Drums, Singing and Ukulele, and the mix is settled by what they ask for in the first term rather than by us.

Music lessons for Coventry schools

Provision usually fails on administration rather than on teaching. A Coventry school gives us a room and a slot; we hold the staffing, the cover, the safeguarding evidence and the billing, and hand the registers back.

Coventry is in West Midlands, and the agreement is the same in every school we work with there.

School band and ensemble in Coventry

Up to five children, one room, one piece of music. A band or a small ensemble is where a child stops practising and starts playing with other people, which is the part a lesson on its own cannot give them - and in Coventry it happens inside the school day like everything else we run.

When a teacher on the Coventry round is ill, the person who covers is already working in the next school along.
