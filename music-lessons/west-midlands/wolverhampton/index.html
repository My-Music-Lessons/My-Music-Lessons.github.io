ONE CLEAR NEXT STEP

From a first conversation to music in a Wolverhampton school.

  1. 01

    One conversation

    You name the school. We find out what is already running there, what the funding allows, and what we could put on next term.

  2. 02

    A room and a slot

    You give us a room and a rotating slot. Nothing else on your timetable changes, and the rotation is what stops one subject losing out.

  3. 03

    We staff it and we cover it

    Our teachers arrive, teach one-to-one, in small groups and in bands of up to five, and we send cover when somebody is ill. Your office is not asked to find anybody.

  4. 04

    The records come back to you

    Registers, practice notes and safeguarding evidence, held by us and open to your team. We bill families directly; you keep the relationship.

WHO PAYS, AND WHEN

Who pays for a place

A place has one payer and never two. Which one applies at a school in Wolverhampton depends on that school and its funding, not on who asks first. Ask and we will tell you what is available at yours.

  • Funded places available

    A school funds a number of places, so those children pay nothing. How many depends on the school and its funding.

  • Family-paid places

    A flat monthly plan, the same every month, with missed lessons made up rather than refunded.

IN MORE DETAIL

More about the service in Wolverhampton

The same service, answered the way a different reader would ask about it. A business manager and a music lead are not asking the same question, and neither of them should have to read past the other one’s answer to find their own.

The music service for Wolverhampton

What a school is actually buying is the absence of a problem: no cover to find, no instruments to insure, no safeguarding file to keep, no invoices to chase. In Wolverhampton that is the whole of our side of it.

Children in Wolverhampton learn Piano, Guitar, Drums, Singing and Ukulele, and the mix is settled by what they ask for in the first term rather than by us.

In-school music service in Wolverhampton

Out of school means the families who cannot get there are out of it. In school means the child whose parents work until six learns an instrument on the same terms as everybody else, and that is most of why we run it this way in Wolverhampton.

The 82 primary schools listed for Wolverhampton come from the Department for Education register rather than from us.

School music lessons in Wolverhampton

A lesson in Wolverhampton is one child, or two or three learning the same thing alongside each other, or up to five playing one piece together. Which of those a child gets depends on the child, not on what is cheapest to run.

In Wolverhampton that covers 82 primary schools, and a head looking for their own will find it listed on this page.

Peripatetic music teachers in Wolverhampton

The word is old and the arrangement is simple: the same teacher comes to your Wolverhampton school every week, knows the children by name, and belongs to a round rather than to a booking.

What a Wolverhampton school provides is a room and a rotating slot. Where it funds places it pays for those, and there is nothing else - no cover to arrange, no instruments to buy, no invoices through your office.

Instrumental tuition in Wolverhampton

Tuition on the instrument the child actually wants, taught by somebody who plays it, inside the Wolverhampton school day.

A lesson in Wolverhampton is one to five children, in term time, inside the school day, and a practice note goes home after it.

Music lessons for Wolverhampton schools

For a school this is provision without administration: we staff it, cover it, record it and bill the families directly. Wolverhampton schools keep their timetable and lose the paperwork.

Wolverhampton sits on the same weekly round as Birmingham, Coventry and Solihull, which is what makes cover possible rather than merely promised.

School band and ensemble in Wolverhampton

Up to five children, one room, one piece of music. A band or a small ensemble is where a child stops practising and starts playing with other people, which is the part a lesson on its own cannot give them - and in Wolverhampton it happens inside the school day like everything else we run.

When a teacher on the Wolverhampton round is ill, the person who covers is already working in the next school along.
