ONE CLEAR NEXT STEP

From a first conversation to music in a Sunderland school.

  1. 01

    One conversation

    You name the school. We find out what is already running there, what the funding allows, and what we could put on next term.

  2. 02

    A room and a slot

    You give us a room and a rotating slot. Nothing else on your timetable changes, and the rotation is what stops one subject losing out.

  3. 03

    We staff it and we cover it

    Our teachers arrive, teach one-to-one, in small groups and in bands of up to five, and we send cover when somebody is ill. Your office is not asked to find anybody.

  4. 04

    The records come back to you

    Registers, practice notes and safeguarding evidence, held by us and open to your team. We bill families directly; you keep the relationship.

WHO PAYS, AND WHEN

Who pays for a place

A place has one payer and never two. Which one applies at a school in Sunderland depends on that school and its funding, not on who asks first. Ask and we will tell you what is available at yours.

  • Funded places available

    A school funds a number of places, so those children pay nothing. How many depends on the school and its funding.

  • Family-paid places

    A flat monthly plan, the same every month, with missed lessons made up rather than refunded.

IN MORE DETAIL

More about the service in Sunderland

The same service, answered the way a different reader would ask about it. A business manager and a music lead are not asking the same question, and neither of them should have to read past the other one’s answer to find their own.

The music service for Sunderland

A music service is the whole thing, not a teacher: staffing, cover when somebody is ill, instruments, records of who played what, and one invoice. That is what we run in Sunderland.

In Sunderland that covers 50 primary schools, and a head looking for their own will find it listed on this page.

In-school music service in Sunderland

Out of school means the families who cannot get there are out of it. In school means the child whose parents work until six learns an instrument on the same terms as everybody else, and that is most of why we run it this way in Sunderland.

What a Sunderland school provides is a room and a rotating slot. Where it funds places it pays for those, and there is nothing else - no cover to arrange, no instruments to buy, no invoices through your office.

School music lessons in Sunderland

One to five children at a time, inside the day. A practice note goes home afterwards, so a parent in Sunderland knows what happened without anybody in your office having to tell them.

Children in Sunderland learn Piano, Guitar, Drums, Singing and Ukulele, and the mix is settled by what they ask for in the first term rather than by us.

Peripatetic music teachers in Sunderland

Our teachers travel to schools across Sunderland on a fixed weekly round, which is what makes cover possible when one of them is ill - the school is never left with an empty room.

Sunderland sits on the same weekly round as Newcastle upon Tyne, Gateshead and South Shields, which is what makes cover possible rather than merely promised.

Instrumental tuition in Sunderland

Tuition rather than an activity: there is a thing to learn, a way of telling whether it is being learnt, and a note home saying what to practise before next week. That is what a Sunderland family is paying for, or what a Sunderland school is funding on their behalf.

Sunderland is in Tyne and Wear, and the agreement is the same in every school we work with there.

Music lessons for Sunderland schools

What we ask of a Sunderland school is deliberately narrow - a room and a rotating slot - and everything after that is ours. No invoices through your office, no cover to find, no instrument cupboard to manage.

A lesson in Sunderland is one to five children, in term time, inside the school day, and a practice note goes home after it.

School band and ensemble in Sunderland

Five is the largest number where everybody still has a part worth playing. A band that size in Sunderland is the point at which a child stops counting bars on their own and starts listening to somebody else.

When a teacher on the Sunderland round is ill, the person who covers is already working in the next school along.
