The Bristol schools we play in

We staff it, cover it and record it. Your school keeps its timetable.

The music in a Bristol school week does not have to be an add-on. We staff it, run it and report on it as part of the ordinary day.

There are 219 primary schools in and around Bristol. The largest is Air Balloon Hill Primary School, with around 790 children, and 66 of them are the size an in-school music service usually runs in.

Teachers, instruments and cover

We staff the lessons and cover them when somebody is ill. Your school does not find a replacement.

Inside the school day

A room and a rotating slot. One-to-one, small groups, and bands of up to five, in term time, so the same subject is never missed twice.

Safeguarding and records held by us

Checks, registers, practice notes and evidence, kept by us and available to your team whenever you ask.

Two payers, and never both

A place is funded by the school or paid for by the family, never part of each. Many schools fund a set number of places and the other families pay. Which applies depends on the school, not on who asks first.

ONE CLEAR NEXT STEP

From a first conversation to music in a Bristol school.

  1. 01

    One conversation

    You name the school. We find out what is already running there, what the funding allows, and what we could put on next term.

  2. 02

    A room and a slot

    You give us a room and a rotating slot. Nothing else on your timetable changes, and the rotation is what stops one subject losing out.

  3. 03

    We staff it and we cover it

    Our teachers arrive, teach one-to-one, in small groups and in bands of up to five, and we send cover when somebody is ill. Your office is not asked to find anybody.

  4. 04

    The records come back to you

    Registers, practice notes and safeguarding evidence, held by us and open to your team. We bill families directly; you keep the relationship.

WHO PAYS, AND WHEN

Who pays for a place

A place has one payer and never two. Which one applies at a school in Bristol depends on that school and its funding, not on who asks first. Ask and we will tell you what is available at yours.

  • Funded places available

    A school funds a number of places, so those children pay nothing. How many depends on the school and its funding.

  • Family-paid places

    A flat monthly plan, the same every month, with missed lessons made up rather than refunded.

IN MORE DETAIL

More about the service in Bristol

The same service, answered the way a different reader would ask about it. A business manager and a music lead are not asking the same question, and neither of them should have to read past the other one’s answer to find their own.

The music service for Bristol

A teacher is one person on one day. A service is what happens when that person is ill, when a child moves up a year, when a parent asks what their money bought, and when somebody asks the school to account for it. We hold all four for Bristol schools.

What a Bristol school provides is a room and a rotating slot. Where it funds places it pays for those, and there is nothing else - no cover to arrange, no instruments to buy, no invoices through your office.

In-school music service in Bristol

Out of school means the families who cannot get there are out of it. In school means the child whose parents work until six learns an instrument on the same terms as everybody else, and that is most of why we run it this way in Bristol.

Bristol is in Bristol, and the agreement is the same in every school we work with there.

School music lessons in Bristol

One to five children at a time, inside the day. A practice note goes home afterwards, so a parent in Bristol knows what happened without anybody in your office having to tell them.

Bristol is in Bristol, and the agreement is the same in every school we work with there.

Peripatetic music teachers in Bristol

The word is old and the arrangement is simple: the same teacher comes to your Bristol school every week, knows the children by name, and belongs to a round rather than to a booking.

Bristol is in Bristol, and the agreement is the same in every school we work with there.

Instrumental tuition in Bristol

Tuition on the instrument the child actually wants, taught by somebody who plays it, inside the Bristol school day.

Bristol sits on the same weekly round as Clifton, Bedminster and Horfield, which is what makes cover possible rather than merely promised.

Music lessons for Bristol schools

Provision usually fails on administration rather than on teaching. A Bristol school gives us a room and a slot; we hold the staffing, the cover, the safeguarding evidence and the billing, and hand the registers back.

In Bristol that covers 219 primary schools, and a head looking for their own will find it listed on this page.
