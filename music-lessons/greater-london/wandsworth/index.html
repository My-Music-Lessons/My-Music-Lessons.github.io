ONE CLEAR NEXT STEP

From a first conversation to music in a Wandsworth school.

  1. 01

    One conversation

    You name the school. We find out what is already running there, what the funding allows, and what we could put on next term.

  2. 02

    A room and a slot

    You give us a room and a rotating slot. Nothing else on your timetable changes, and the rotation is what stops one subject losing out.

  3. 03

    We staff it and we cover it

    Our teachers arrive, teach one-to-one, in small groups and in bands of up to five, and we send cover when somebody is ill. Your office is not asked to find anybody.

  4. 04

    The records come back to you

    Registers, practice notes and safeguarding evidence, held by us and open to your team. We bill families directly; you keep the relationship.

WHO PAYS, AND WHEN

Who pays for a place

A place has one payer and never two. Which one applies at a school in Wandsworth depends on that school and its funding, not on who asks first. Ask and we will tell you what is available at yours.

  • Funded places available

    A school funds a number of places, so those children pay nothing. How many depends on the school and its funding.

  • Family-paid places

    A flat monthly plan, the same every month, with missed lessons made up rather than refunded.

The music service for Wandsworth

A teacher is one person on one day. A service is what happens when that person is ill, when a child moves up a year, when a parent asks what their money bought, and when somebody asks the school to account for it. We hold all four for Wandsworth schools.

Wandsworth sits on the same weekly round as Croydon, Bromley and Ealing, which is what makes cover possible rather than merely promised.

In-school music service in Wandsworth

Nothing happens after the bell. A child in Wandsworth walks to the room, has their lesson and walks back, and the only thing that changes on your timetable is that a room is booked.

Children in Wandsworth learn Piano, Guitar, Drums, Singing and Ukulele, and the mix is settled by what they ask for in the first term rather than by us.

School music lessons in Wandsworth

A lesson in Wandsworth is one child, or two or three learning the same thing alongside each other, or up to five playing one piece together. Which of those a child gets depends on the child, not on what is cheapest to run.

Wandsworth is in Greater London, and the agreement is the same in every school we work with there.

Peripatetic music teachers in Wandsworth

The word is old and the arrangement is simple: the same teacher comes to your Wandsworth school every week, knows the children by name, and belongs to a round rather than to a booking.

In Wandsworth that covers 58 primary schools, and a head looking for their own will find it listed on this page.

Instrumental tuition in Wandsworth

A child who chose their instrument practises. A child who was handed one usually does not. So tuition in Wandsworth starts from what the child asked for, taught by somebody whose own instrument it is.

The 58 primary schools listed for Wandsworth come from the Department for Education register rather than from us.

Music lessons for Wandsworth schools

What we ask of a Wandsworth school is deliberately narrow - a room and a rotating slot - and everything after that is ours. No invoices through your office, no cover to find, no instrument cupboard to manage.

A lesson in Wandsworth is one to five children, in term time, inside the school day, and a practice note goes home after it.

School band and ensemble in Wandsworth

Five is the largest number where everybody still has a part worth playing. A band that size in Wandsworth is the point at which a child stops counting bars on their own and starts listening to somebody else.

When a teacher on the Wandsworth round is ill, the person who covers is already working in the next school along.
