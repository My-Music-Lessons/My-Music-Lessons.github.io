Music lessons across Derbyshire

Derbyshire is the edge of the Peak District, where half the county is rural and half is not. We run an in-school music service in 9 towns and cities here, with free, subsidised and family-paid places depending on the school.

  • Government-backed provisionDelivered with music education hubs across England
