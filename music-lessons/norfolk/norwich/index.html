Teachers, instruments and cover

We staff the lessons and cover them when somebody is ill. Your school does not find a replacement.

Inside the school day

A room and a rotating slot. One-to-one, small groups, and bands of up to five, in term time, so the same subject is never missed twice.

Safeguarding and records held by us

Checks, registers, practice notes and evidence, kept by us and available to your team whenever you ask.

Two payers, and never both

A place is funded by the school or paid for by the family, never part of each. Many schools fund a set number of places and the other families pay. Which applies depends on the school, not on who asks first.

ONE CLEAR NEXT STEP

From a first conversation to music in a Norwich school.

  1. 01

    One conversation

    You name the school. We find out what is already running there, what the funding allows, and what we could put on next term.

  2. 02

    A room and a slot

    You give us a room and a rotating slot. Nothing else on your timetable changes, and the rotation is what stops one subject losing out.

  3. 03

    We staff it and we cover it

    Our teachers arrive, teach one-to-one, in small groups and in bands of up to five, and we send cover when somebody is ill. Your office is not asked to find anybody.

  4. 04

    The records come back to you

    Registers, practice notes and safeguarding evidence, held by us and open to your team. We bill families directly; you keep the relationship.

WHO PAYS, AND WHEN

Who pays for a place

A place has one payer and never two. Which one applies at a school in Norwich depends on that school and its funding, not on who asks first. Ask and we will tell you what is available at yours.

  • Funded places available

    A school funds a number of places, so those children pay nothing. How many depends on the school and its funding.

  • Family-paid places

    A flat monthly plan, the same every month, with missed lessons made up rather than refunded.

More about the service in Norwich

The same service, answered the way a different reader would ask about it. A business manager and a music lead are not asking the same question, and neither of them should have to read past the other one’s answer to find their own.

The music service for Norwich

A music service is the whole thing, not a teacher: staffing, cover when somebody is ill, instruments, records of who played what, and one invoice. That is what we run in Norwich.

Children here learn Piano, Guitar, Drums, Singing and Ukulele, and the mix is settled by what they ask for in the first term rather than by us.

In-school music service in Norwich

Out of school means the families who cannot get there are out of it. In school means the child whose parents work until six learns an instrument on the same terms as everybody else, and that is most of why we run it this way in Norwich.

Norwich sits on the same weekly round as Great Yarmouth, King's Lynn and Thetford, which is what makes cover possible rather than merely promised.

School music lessons in Norwich

One to five children at a time, inside the day. A practice note goes home afterwards, so a parent in Norwich knows what happened without anybody in your office having to tell them.

Norwich is in Norfolk, and the agreement is the same in every school we work with there.

Peripatetic music teachers in Norwich

The word is old and the arrangement is simple: the same teacher comes to your Norwich school every week, knows the children by name, and belongs to a round rather than to a booking.

In Norwich that covers 142 primary schools, and a head looking for their own will find it listed on this page.

Instrumental tuition in Norwich

A child who chose their instrument practises. A child who was handed one usually does not. So tuition in Norwich starts from what the child asked for, taught by somebody whose own instrument it is.

Norwich is in Norfolk, and the agreement is the same in every school we work with there.

Music lessons for Norwich schools

What we ask of a Norwich school is deliberately narrow - a room and a rotating slot - and everything after that is ours. No invoices through your office, no cover to find, no instrument cupboard to manage.
